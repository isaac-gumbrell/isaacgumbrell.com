# Home (hero) surface brief

Scope: `/` home page, plus the shared site chrome it establishes (header, footer, tokens) that the Market Watch, Projects and Repositories pages inherit.
Visitor mode: Persuade (home). Market Watch pages are Operate and inherit the world with restraint.

Audience and job: clients and employers judging credibility (then emailing); energy peers returning for Market Watch.
Action: email Isaac (primary); open Market Watch (secondary, one click).
Proof on hand: seven real projects with clients, years and roles (`_pages/projects.md`); live Market Watch feed; open-source repos. No metrics or testimonials.
Steer from Isaac: the focus is modelling combined with qualitative work, not modelling alone.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a merit order. Isaac's real projects stack as supply steps in date order, each step filled solid for modelling and hatched for qualitative work (research, review, report drafting), and the visitor's question is the demand curve that clears them. It refuses the consultant default: headshot beside a headline over three service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white chart paper (no cream), ink axes and hairline gridlines, one deep-teal ramp for steps with diagonal hatch for qualitative work, signal orange reserved for the demand curve, clearing point and actions only. Archivo (condensed width for axes, labels, numbers; tabular figures) carries the quantitative voice; Source Serif 4 carries prose, the qualitative voice. No shadows: depth from overlap and line weight. One status ramp for feed states.

STORY: In seconds the visitor sees that Isaac builds the models and writes the advice, for Energy Policy WA, AEMO, Transpower and IESO; they email him, or open Market Watch.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Upper band: headline and subline on the left seven columns with Email (orange, filled) and Market Watch (ink outline) beneath; portrait in a ruled rectangular frame on the right three columns. Lower band, full width: the merit-order chart, about 300px tall, seven project steps on a labelled axis, legend (Modelling solid, Qualitative hatched), orange demand curve crossing at a marked clearing point. Hover or focus on a step names the project, client, years and roles.

FORM: Merit Order, position 1 on my ordered list (Impeccable's pick, chosen by Isaac over the assigned Drawing Set). seed:6163ea40c198a76e

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Raises kept from the declined hand: orange only where something can be done or is due (Warm Consumer App); no shadows, depth by overlap (Papercut); every element on one column grid (Signal Bench); one status ramp (Cracktro); one axis through the visit, hero to tools to work to contact (Versailles).
